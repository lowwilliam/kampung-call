# Full asset visual review — 2026-08-14

## Scope and acceptance rule

All 68 canonical catalogue GLBs were inspected one by one in deterministic front, rear, left and right views. Technical validity and visual validity were scored separately. A visual pass requires a coherent silhouette, attached parts, readable material zones, and believable side/rear treatment at the catalogue camera distance. Named Singapore landmarks also require their identity-defining features to survive the stylised low-poly treatment.

The practical acceptance threshold is 0.70. Scores are reconstruction-fidelity judgements for this real-time art direction, not claims of survey accuracy. Hidden elevations remain explicitly inferred where no authoritative view establishes them.
