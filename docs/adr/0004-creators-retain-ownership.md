# Let contributors retain ownership and grant display rights

Contributors retain ownership of their models and give the Showcase only a revocable, non-exclusive Display Grant; public downloads are disabled. Administrators may curate Public Copy and viewer presentation but cannot alter the GLB. A valid Submission Receipt can withdraw pending work or unpublish a Community Asset immediately, with model deletion after a seven-day recovery window, while reports can independently trigger review without erasing the audit record.
