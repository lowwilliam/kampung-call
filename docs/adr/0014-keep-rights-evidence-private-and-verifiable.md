# Keep rights evidence private and verifiable

Original licences, assignments, purchase records, permissions, releases, and legal opinions will live in a private encrypted evidence store rather than Git or email alone. The public Catalogue Manifest exposes only the clearance status, scope, reviewer, review date, and evidence hash; high-risk assets require review by a Singapore intellectual-property professional before Display Clearance or a Download Grant, while each release also receives an independent keyboard, screen-reader, and mobile accessibility check.
