# Publish from a versioned catalogue manifest

The public Showcase will treat a repository-owned, versioned Catalogue Manifest as the canonical record for every published Game Asset. Each record must contain a stable ID and URL, locale-ready display and story fields, category, Curated Order, GLB checksum and version, a checksum-bound Card Preview, Creator Credit, adapters, Production Method, Asset Provenance, Singapore Connection, accessible historical sources, separately documented rights evidence for any source photographs or plans, Evidence Status for cultural, historical, and reconstruction claims, publication state, last review date, Display Clearance, a Responsible Publisher with a public LinkedIn profile or official institutional website, and a separately evaluated Download Grant identifying the source URL, licence, and reuse conditions. The initial Responsible Publisher is William Liu, identified by `https://www.linkedin.com/in/ruiqian-liu/`. English is the first public locale; translations remain unpublished until human review. Game audits, Lost Heritage data, and databases may supply or validate inputs, but they do not independently define what appears in a release; an asset becomes public only through a validated manifest revision so each Catalogue Release is reproducible and traceable. Every change must pass schema, rights, provenance, model, preview, link, accessibility, and performance validation and produce a reviewable diff before William Liu approves it. A rights, provenance, or factual-confidence failure converts the record into a visible Withdrawn Asset while stopping model and download distribution immediately; the audit record may follow during emergency withdrawal. The public Collection release remains blocked until all 68 Game Assets have Display Clearance; download availability may differ per asset.
