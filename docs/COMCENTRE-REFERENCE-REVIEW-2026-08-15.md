## Reconstruction target

This is a stylized real-time reconstruction, not a surveyed replica. The target is a recognisable lifecycle portrait of Comcentre: the late-life pale tower and Singtel crown are combined with the historically important rooftop microwave installation. Hidden elevations, exact equipment geometry, and lifecycle-specific dish placement remain approximate.

## Reference set

- [Roots surveyed-site record](https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/places/surveyed-sites/comcentre): podium, entrance, plaza, and late-life street context.
- [Singtel redevelopment announcement](https://www.singtel.com/about-us/media-centre/news-releases/singtel-to-redevelop-comcentre-headquarters--into-next-generatio): official aerial context and the building's telecommunications significance.
- [Singtel 145th-anniversary feature](https://www.singtel.com/about-us/media-centre/news-releases/singtel-launches-voices-from-comcentre-to-mark-145th-anniversary): official historical context.

- [CTBUH / Skyscraper Center](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/singapore/comcentre/10199): long-elevation proportions, recessed office grid, service-core width, and crown hierarchy.
- [National Archives of Singapore record](https://www.nas.gov.sg/archivesonline/photographs/record-details/f16c56da-1161-11e3-83d5-0050568939ad): historical record cross-check.

Reference images are research inputs only and are not redistributed with the model.

## Quality contract

The asset must preserve these identity features at catalogue-card distance:

1. A tall, narrow rectilinear slab framed by broad opaque side cores.
2. A dense recessed office-window grid, not isolated dark stripes.
3. A stepped crown with upper slit, logo panel, dark bands, and a sloped terrace/vent layer.
4. A full-width open rooftop space frame with recognisable parabolic dishes.
5. A substantial two-wing, five-storey podium with horizontal equipment bands.
6. A curved glazed central entrance and plaza that establish the front orientation.

## Review and correction

The previous asset failed the contract in the crown and roof silhouette: three sphere-like forms stood in for the dishes, the open steel frame was absent, the crown was a plain box, the façade rhythm was too coarse, and the podium and side elevations lacked articulation.

The rebuild introduced semantic component groups for the service cores, 25-band front office grid, rear ribbons, stepped telecommunications crown, procedural Singtel wordmark, two-wing equipment podium, glazed entrance rotunda, full X-braced roof lattice, and six procedural parabolic dishes. A second orbit review corrected the blank side view with a restrained service reveal and panel seams, carried podium bands around the side faces, and increased the office-band density from 20 to 25.
