# Kampung Call Showcase

The language of the standalone, player-facing experience that presents the 3D world of Kampung Call.

## Language

**Visitor**:
A player or prospective player exploring the Showcase and the stories behind the game's world.
_Avoid_: Client, stakeholder, buyer

**Showcase**:
A standalone website for exploring the 3D assets and stories of Kampung Call, separate from the playable game.
_Avoid_: Game homepage, in-game gallery

**Asset Provenance**:
The origin and attribution context of an asset, including whether it was created for Kampung Call or derived from a third-party source.
_Avoid_: Ownership

**Collection Asset**:
A publicly visible 3D model in either the official game collection or the approved community collection.
_Avoid_: Submission, source file, planned asset

**Game Asset**:
One of the 55 production GLB models used by Kampung Call and available for interactive viewing in the Showcase. Planned vendor models without viewable files are not Game Assets.
_Avoid_: Community Asset, every repository file

**Community Asset**:
An approved Singapore-related contribution published in the Showcase's community collection. Publication does not imply that the asset appears in Kampung Call.
_Avoid_: Game Asset, Submission

**Asset Card**:
The primary browsing unit in the Showcase, presenting one Collection Asset as a product-like object that can be examined in 360 degrees.
_Avoid_: Map marker, island location

**Collection**:
The card-first presentation of all Collection Assets, without an island, world map, or spatial navigation metaphor.
_Avoid_: Island atlas, world map

**Display Name**:
The fictional in-game name used as a Collection Asset's primary title. A recognisable real-world inspiration may appear secondarily when it is clearly labelled and sourced.
_Avoid_: Official name, replica name

**Live Preview**:
The auto-rotating 360-degree presentation within an Asset Card that lets a Visitor take control by dragging or swiping.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, turntable video

**Detail View**:
The expanded presentation of a selected Collection Asset, including its larger 360-degree view and complete Asset Story.
_Avoid_: Product checkout, game scene

**Studio Stage**:
The warm, editorial display surface that isolates a Collection Asset for close inspection while retaining Kampung Call's chalk, ink, teal, and terracotta identity.
_Avoid_: Island scene, dark technical viewport

## Contributions

**Contributor**:
A Visitor who submits a Singapore-related 3D model without creating an account. The Contributor's name is public credit; an optional LinkedIn profile is public only with explicit display consent.
_Avoid_: Authenticated user, Game developer, vendor

**Submission**:
A Contributor's self-contained GLB and its supporting Singapore context, provenance, rights attestation, and optional LinkedIn profile while it awaits an Administrator's decision. A Submission is not publicly visible; approval turns it into a Community Asset.
_Avoid_: Collection Asset, published asset

**Administrator**:
A trusted operator who signs into the password-protected admin portal, reviews Submissions, and decides whether they may appear publicly in the Showcase.
_Avoid_: Contributor, automated approver

**Review State**:
The moderation position of a Submission: `Submitted`, `Needs review`, `Changes requested`, `Rejected`, `Published`, or `Unpublished`. Only `Published` creates a publicly visible Community Asset.
_Avoid_: Upload progress, automated validation result

**Submission Receipt**:
A private link and recovery code that let an account-free Contributor revisit a Submission, read review notes, provide a replacement model, withdraw pending work, or request unpublishing.
_Avoid_: User account, public asset link

**Quarantine**:
Private storage for submitted models before publication. Files in Quarantine have no public URL and rejected files expire after 30 days.
_Avoid_: Community collection, public asset storage

**Display Grant**:
The Contributor's revocable, non-exclusive permission for the Showcase to store, render, resize, promote, and display a Submission without transferring ownership or enabling model downloads.
_Avoid_: Ownership transfer, unrestricted licence

**Public Copy**:
The title, description, categories, tags, attribution, and viewer presentation shown for a Community Asset. An Administrator may curate Public Copy but may not alter the contributed GLB.
_Avoid_: Source model, private review notes

**Asset Report**:
A Visitor's notice that a Community Asset may have incorrect attribution, a rights problem, or inappropriate content. A report can lead to unpublishing without erasing the review record.
_Avoid_: Review note, automatic deletion

**Singapore Connection**:
A meaningful relationship between a Community Asset's subject or story and Singapore. The Contributor's residence does not determine eligibility.
_Avoid_: Upload location, creator nationality

**Featured Community Asset**:
A published Community Asset deliberately elevated by an Administrator ahead of the remaining community collection, which otherwise appears newest first.
_Avoid_: Most liked, paid placement

## Story Layers

**Singapore Context**:
Sourced cultural or historical context connecting a Collection Asset to Singapore.
_Avoid_: Lore, flavour text

**Game Context**:
The role a Collection Asset plays in the world or narrative of Kampung Call.
_Avoid_: Real-world history

**Production Story**:
How and why a Collection Asset was made or adapted, including its Asset Provenance and art-direction choices.
_Avoid_: Singapore Context

**Asset Story**:
The complete editorial treatment of a Collection Asset. Its depth follows the asset's nature: cultural assets receive Singapore Context, residents receive biographies, and everyday props receive concise observational and production stories.
_Avoid_: Uniform article, invented history
