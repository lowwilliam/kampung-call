# Singapore fauna and transport asset research

Date: 2026-08-15
Target: real-time browser props, procedural source, editable Blender files, GLB delivery
Fidelity statement: stylized reference-led reconstructions. The supplied photographs do not reveal every hidden surface, so the unseen sides and appendage cross-sections are anatomical or mechanical inferences rather than exact recovery.

## Why these five assets

| Priority | Asset | Why it belongs in the library | Identity system that must survive simplification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Smooth-coated otter (`Lutrogale perspicillata`) | A highly recognisable Singapore urban-waterway animal and a useful small-mammal rig base. | Long streamlined torso, broad flattened tail, rounded head, short ears, webbed feet, brown upper coat and buff throat/underside. |
| 2 | Red junglefowl (`Gallus gallus`) | The local “wild chicken” is common in parks yet morphologically distinct from a generic farm rooster. | Slate-grey legs, white ear patch and rump, orange hackles, red comb/wattles, dark iridescent arched tail. |
| 3 | Oriental pied hornbill (`Anthracoceros albirostris`) | A Singapore conservation-success icon and a strong canopy silhouette. | Oversized pale curved bill, black-tipped casque, black upperparts, white belly/undertail, long tail, red eye on the male. |
| 4 | Clouded monitor (`Varanus nebulosus`) | A native forest-floor reptile that broadens the collection beyond mammals and birds. | Shorter narrow head, nostril midway between eye and snout, strong limbs and claws, rounded tail, grey-brown body with dense yellow spots. |
| 5 | Singapore Cable Car SkyOrb cabin | A Sentosa/Mount Faber transport landmark with a distinct current-generation form. | Chrome-finished spherical shell, panoramic circular window ring, glazed floor, roof hanger/grip, ventilation louvres and six-seat interior. |

NParks describes smooth-coated otters as reaching roughly 1.2 m, with fully webbed feet and a flattened tail. Red junglefowl are distinguished locally by slate-grey legs, a white rump and white ear patch. The Oriental pied hornbill is approximately 69–70 cm and is defined by its pale bill, casque and black/white plumage. The clouded monitor reaches about 1–1.5 m and is differentiated from the water monitor by its shorter head, mid-snout nostril and rounder tail. Mount Faber Leisure documents the 2024 SkyOrb as a chrome-finished spherical cabin with a glass floor and illuminated window ring; reporting at launch gives an approximate 2.5 m height and 2.3 m width.

## Scope decision

These are standalone library props rather than scene dioramas. Animals ship in neutral, readable poses with named limb/head/tail or wing pivots. The cable car ships as the cabin and hanger assembly, not as a complete cableway tower system. Logos, campaign wraps and passengers are intentionally omitted; the cabin is an approximate unbranded reconstruction of the industrial form.
